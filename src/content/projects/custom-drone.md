## What I built

Designed and 3D-printed a custom 5-inch freestyle quadcopter airframe (MK5X) in CAD, and soldered and assembled the full electronics stack, flying it with a RadioMaster transmitter.

## Technical details

- Flight controller / ESC: DolphinRC F405 flight controller paired with a 60A ESC.
- Motors: MEPS Neon 2207 2050KV brushless motors.
- Props: HQProp E-thix S5, 5-inch.
- Video: DJI O3 digital FPV system.
- Receiver: SoloGood ExpressLRS (ELRS) receiver, paired to a RadioMaster transmitter.
- Battery: Ovonic 6S 1300mAh LiPo.
