## Overview

A rover built for McGill's ECSE 211 Design Project, programmed in Python on a Raspberry Pi to navigate autonomously using sensor feedback and closed-loop control.

## The problem

The course's four labs and final project required a rover that could sense its environment and respond with correct, repeatable motion — built, tested, and documented by a full team on a tight schedule.

## What I built

Led a team of six through four labs and a final project, building Gantt charts and writing the technical reports along the way. The rover combines ultrasonic and light sensors, wheel encoders, and a conveyor-belt mechanism, controlled from a Raspberry Pi.

## Technical details

Programmed bang-bang and P controllers, odometry, and finite-state machines in Python to drive the rover's behavior and track its position in real time.

## Results

Completed all four labs and the final project with the full team.
